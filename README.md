## About

I'm **Hossam Gouda**, an agricultural engineer with **10+ years in irrigation systems, water infrastructure and agricultural projects**, based in **Jazan, Saudi Arabia**. By day I run a branch for an industrial piping and irrigation supplier, handling key accounts with EPC contractors and export sales. Outside that, I build software for the engineering work I know well: hydraulic design, irrigation scheduling, field measurement and crop monitoring.

- 💧 **Domain:** drip & sprinkler design, HDPE/PVC pipe networks, pump systems, fertigation, ET/Kc water budgeting
- 🛠️ **Building:** engineering software for irrigation design, water management and crop monitoring
- 🎓 **Education:** Executive MBA, Quantic School of Business & Technology (2026) · B.Sc. Agricultural Engineering, Cairo University
- 🤝 **Open to:** irrigation / water / piping sales leadership roles in the GCC, and AgTech product collaborations
